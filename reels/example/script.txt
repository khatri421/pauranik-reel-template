# JAMBAVAN KI ICHCHHA · BHAAG 1
Lanka ki yuddh-bhoomi shaant ho chuki thi.

Par Jambavan ke mann mein ek ichchha baaki thi.

Woh dheere-dheere Shri Ram ke paas pahunche.

Haath jodkar bole, "Prabhu,

ek baar aapse dwandva yuddh karna hai."

Shri Ram ne muskurakar haath uthaya.

"Abhi nahi, Jambavan.

Agle avatar mein ye ichchha poori karunga."

Jambavan ne intezaar shuru kiya...

aur phir Dwarka mein Krishna aaye.
